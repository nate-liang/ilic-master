# IIC design system

Editorial competition identity inspired by Fixaplan's mixed typography, generous scrolling, and layered narrative. Preserve Instrument Serif and Inter, with Georgia and sans-serif fallbacks. Forest green #17432F anchors the canvas, #2C6147 supplies brand accents, and cream #F5F3EB supplies reading surfaces. All supplied logo PNGs are copied unchanged into assets; do not recolor, crop, or redraw them.

The home page pairs a large typographic composition with the complete ivy logo and thin orbital lines. Content pages use broad headings, divided rows, portrait-led team sections, and sponsor tiers. Use ordinary document scrolling. At 800px navigation becomes an expandable panel; at 420px team profiles stack. Keep readable type and visible keyboard focus.

Motion: a clipped heading entrance, logo settle, one-time scroll reveals, and accordion height transitions. Reduced motion disables all animation, transition, and smooth scrolling. Content is visible without JavaScript. Avoid adding libraries for decorative motion.
