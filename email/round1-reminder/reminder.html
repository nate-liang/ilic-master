What to submit

An anonymized investment memo (PDF) and financial model. Enter your registered Team ID and stock in the submission form. Sign in to Google to upload your files.

  • Memo body: 1,500 words maximum, excluding exhibits, footnotes, and appendix.
  • Entire memo: 15 pages maximum, including the one-page cover, exhibits, footnotes, and appendix.
  • Formatting: PDF; minimum 11-point font; standard margins.
  • Memo filename: TEAMID_TICKER_LONG.pdf or TEAMID_TICKER_SHORT.pdf.
  • Anonymous review: use your registered Team ID only. No school names, team member names, logos, or identifying metadata anywhere in either file.

Include on your cover page

  • Long or short; company name, ticker, and exchange.
  • Current price and reference date; market capitalization and enterprise value.
  • Price target and IRR over your stated holding period.
  • A three-line thesis summary.

Team eligibility

Registered investment/finance clubs at the eight Ivy League institutions may enter two teams per school in Round 1; one team per school advances to the final rounds.

Each team must have 4 currently enrolled undergraduates, with at most 2 seniors, at least 1 sophomore, and at least 1 male and 1 female. All members must be listed at registration. Substitutions after the Round 1 deadline require committee approval.

Faculty and alumni may advise on process and provide feedback, but may not write, edit, or build any part of the submission. Disclose each advisor’s name and role.
